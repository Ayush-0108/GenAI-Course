Cricket is a popular sport played by two teams with a ball and bat. The objective of the game is to score runs by hitting the ball with the bat. The team with the most runs at the end of the game wins. Cricket is played on a rectangular field with a flat surface. The field has a pitch in the center, where the ball is bowled. The pitch is 22 yards long and 3.33 yards wide. The ball is hard and made of leather, with a cork core. The bat is flat and made of wood, with a long handle.

The game starts with one team batting and the other team bowling. The batting team sends two batsmen onto the field. The bowling team sends one bowler onto the field. The bowler delivers the ball to the batsman. The batsman attempts to hit the ball with the bat. If the batsman hits the ball, they can score runs. The batsman can run to the opposite end of the pitch to score a run. The batsman can also hit boundaries, which are runs scored when the ball reaches the edge of the field. A boundary is worth four runs. If the ball is hit out of the field, it is a six, which is worth six runs.

The batting team continues to bat until all the batsmen are out. A batsman can get out in several ways, including being bowled, caught, or run out. If a batsman is bowled, it means the ball has hit the wickets and knocked them over. If a batsman is caught, it means a fielder has caught the ball hit by the batsman. If a batsman is run out, it means a fielder has hit the wickets while the batsman is running. The bowling team tries to get the batsmen out to limit the runs scored.
